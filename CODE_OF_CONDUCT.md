# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes respectful, evidence-aware collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss claims and trade-offs without attacking people.
- Preserve material dissent instead of pressuring contributors into artificial consensus.
- Distinguish facts, inferences, preferences, and unresolved questions.
- Protect private prompts, personal data, credentials, and confidential project details.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and credit sources and contributors.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate deception, and publishing another person's confidential material are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a concern that is safe to discuss publicly, open an Issue with only the minimum necessary detail. For a sensitive concern, use a private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile and do not disclose details publicly. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as the available channel allows.
