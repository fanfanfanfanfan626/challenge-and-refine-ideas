# Idea Council examples

These copy-paste requests show when the Skill is useful and what a good result contains. They are illustrative, not testimonials or evidence of real-world outcomes.

## Explore without forcing a decision

```text
Use $challenge-and-refine-ideas to explore a privacy-first study-planning app. Do not recommend a business model yet. Confirm the live problem frame, identify materially different interpretations, and ask the question most likely to change the direction.
```

A useful response keeps the session intent as `explore`, states a provisional frame, opens distinct possibilities, and leaves the human disposition `undecided`.

## Compare two live alternatives

```text
Use $challenge-and-refine-ideas to compare a browser-only implementation with a hosted account. Use privacy, maintenance cost, collaboration and offline use as criteria. Separate known facts from assumptions and preserve unresolved objections.
```

A useful response compares the current alternatives against human-owned criteria, labels evidence gaps, and does not turn adviser agreement into user or market evidence.

## Challenge the current candidate

```text
Use $challenge-and-refine-ideas to challenge the latest proposal below. Steelman it first, then attack that exact version. Give every material objection a stable ID and state what evidence would resolve it.
```

An illustrative result may include:

```text
Maturity: challenged
Evidence state: test-needed
Human disposition: undecided
O-001 — Offline recovery is unspecified — open
O-002 — The cost estimate lacks usage data — evidence-needed
Next reversible step: test recovery with three representative failure cases
```

## High-stakes boundary

```text
Use $challenge-and-refine-ideas to review an AI feature that may make medical recommendations to children. Identify what can be discussed now and what requires professional, legal, consent and safety review. Do not hand it to implementation.
```

The Skill may improve the problem frame, but agent consensus cannot satisfy those external gates. A consequential handoff requires evidence sufficient for the exact scope and explicit human authorization.
